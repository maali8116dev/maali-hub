# Application Review Process

The review process begins when an applicant submits a non-draft application, which automatically triggers the assignment of 2-3 reviewers based on the project's category. The system uses a workload-balanced assignment algorithm that selects reviewers who are qualified for the application's category, have the lowest current workload, and have no declared conflicts of interest with the application. Once assigned, reviewers receive notifications and can access the application through their reviewer dashboard. Each reviewer independently evaluates the application using category-specific rubrics, submitting scores across multiple criteria (such as innovation, feasibility, impact, and team quality), an overall score, a recommendation (approve, reject, or request more information), and optional comments. All individual reviewer decisions are tracked and preserved in the system, providing a complete audit trail of each reviewer's assessment. After all reviewers have submitted their evaluations, a final decision is made by a reviewer or administrator who reviews the aggregated recommendations and scores, then approves or rejects the application. The final decision, along with all individual reviewer recommendations, scores, comments, and timestamps, is permanently recorded in the system, ensuring full transparency and accountability throughout the review process.
