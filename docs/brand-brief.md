# Maali — Logo & Brand Brief (for Designer)

## What Maali is

**Maali Opportunity Hub** is a digital platform that connects **African talent** — entrepreneurs, **professionals**, **students**, and **interns** — with **opportunities** across the continent. It is not limited to funding: programs include **grants and funding**, **jobs**, **internships**, **trainings**, **scholarships**, **fellowships**, **accelerators**, **incubators**, **competitions**, **hackathons**, and other partner-listed programs.

**Sectors:** Agriculture, business, technology, fintech, social enterprise, and more (as defined by program partners).

**Tagline (in product today):** _Empowering African Talent_ — logo/wordmark may stay “Maali”; broader positioning below is the strategic direction.

**One-line pitch:** One place to discover, apply for, and track opportunities across Africa — whether you are building a venture, advancing a career, or starting out as an intern or student.

**Positioning:** Maali is more than a listing site. It is a trusted bridge between **applicants** (founders, professionals, interns, students), **program partners**, **reviewers**, and **admins** — built for clarity, speed, and integrity.

---

## Name & meaning (logo context)

- **Maali** — short, memorable, easy to say in English and African languages.
- In **Somali**, _maali_ relates to **wealth / property / value** — aligns with economic opportunity and career growth, not only grants.
- Avoid clichéd “Africa map” or generic safari imagery; prefer **growth, connection, trust, and forward momentum**.
- Full product name when needed: **Maali Opportunity Hub** (logo mark can be **Maali** alone).

---

## Mission & values (tone for the mark)

**Mission:** Democratize access to **opportunities** — funding, jobs, learning, and career pathways — for African entrepreneurs, professionals, students, and interns, fostering innovation and sustainable economic growth across the continent.

**Brand values (from product copy):**

| Value             | Idea for visual language                         |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Empowerment**   | Uplift, access, doors opening                    |
| **Innovation**    | Modern, clear, purposeful                        |
| **Collaboration** | Connection, ecosystem, partnership               |
| **Impact**        | Growth, roots + progress (not charity aesthetic) |

**Tone:** Warm, professional, optimistic, grounded — **not** corporate-cold, **not** playful-startup cartoon. Should feel credible to employers and funders, and welcoming to students, interns, and first-time applicants.

- **Primary applicants:**
  - **Entrepreneurs & founders** — startups, SMEs, NGOs (grants, accelerators, competitions, etc.)

### Signature gradients (used in hero & marketing UI)

- **Hero gradient:** `135deg` — primary dark → sunset red → golden orange  
  (`hsl(15, 85%, 35%)` → `hsl(5, 75%, 55%)` → `hsl(35, 85%, 55%)`)
- **Primary gradient:** terra cotta → golden orange
- **Accent gradient:** vegetation green → teal-green

**Logo guidance:** Primary mark should work in **terra cotta (#C94E1F)** on light backgrounds, and in **white or warm cream** on primary-dark / gradient backgrounds. Optional secondary accent: **green (#33A05A)** for a two-color lockup — not required if a single strong mark is clearer.

**Background (required):** Deliver logo files with a **transparent background** (PNG/SVG). No baked-in white, cream, or colored boxes behind the mark — it must sit cleanly on web headers, emails, PDFs, and partner co-branding on any surface color.

---

## Typography (digital product — for consistency)

- **UI fonts:** System sans stack (`system-ui`, platform native sans) — logo need not match a custom typeface.
- **Headlines in marketing:** Bold, clean sans; gradient text often uses the hero gradient on key words (e.g. “African”, “Opportunities”).
- **Corner radius in UI:** ~12px (`0.75rem`) — soft, modern, not sharp corporate.

## What to avoid

- Stock “Africa outline” clichés without a clear concept.
- Cold fintech blue/purple palette (off-brand).
- Overly childish or mascot-heavy styles.
- Marks that only work in full color (must have a solid one-color version).
- Opaque or off-white boxes behind the logo (use **transparent** exports only).
